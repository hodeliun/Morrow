## What to build

Make formula editing contextual and visual: recognize fields/functions/tables as highlighted references, suggest members after a dot, show descriptions and previews, and provide explicit save/cancel without losing free text editing.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Suggestions include current row columns, available tables and supported functions; dot completion follows a relation target schema.
- [ ] Names with spaces, pasted text, renamed columns and keyboard selection resolve to stable bindings.
- [ ] Recognized references are visually distinct without introducing a second persisted formula language; errors and types remain readable.
- [ ] Formula editing works with empty tables and keyboard/screen reader controls.

## Blocked by

None — can start immediately.
